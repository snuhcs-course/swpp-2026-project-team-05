# Iteration 3 demo recording record

The submitted Android screen recording is stored at
[`demo/iteration-3-demo.mp4`](../demo/iteration-3-demo.mp4). It is a silent,
portrait H.264 video recorded from the working demo and runs for approximately
1 minute 47 seconds.

## Demonstrated flow

1. Open the signed-in news app and manage followed sources.
2. Browse the curated Politics and Society feed and open an article.
3. Read the stored article, save it, and enable expression analysis.
4. Select a marked expression to open the compact explanation.
5. Expand the comparison and move through grounded passages from other
   publishers.
6. Open an original source, return to FrameLESS, and confirm the article in
   Saved Articles.

The recording demonstrates the product flow; it is not the test oracle. The
frozen six-article release smoke, exact success criteria, and CI evidence remain
in [`specs/testing/iteration-3-release-smoke.spec.md`](../specs/testing/iteration-3-release-smoke.spec.md)
and [`docs/testing-documentation.md`](testing-documentation.md).

## Recording safety

- No password, API key, debug menu, or notification content is intentionally
  shown.
- The video does not claim that FrameLESS decides which publisher is correct.
- Source links remain visible so the compared passages can be traced back to
  their articles.
